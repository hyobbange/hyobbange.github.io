# Moving Hidden Object Games: Searching a Crowd That Walks

<p class="lang-switch">English · <a href="{{ '/oddball/ja/moving-crowd/' | relative_url }}" lang="ja">日本語</a> · <a href="{{ '/oddball/es/moving-crowd/' | relative_url }}" lang="es">Español</a> · <a href="{{ '/oddball/pt/moving-crowd/' | relative_url }}" lang="pt-BR">Português</a> · <a href="{{ '/oddball/ko/moving-crowd/' | relative_url }}" lang="ko">한국어</a></p>

A moving hidden object game is a search where **the picture keeps changing while you look**. In a still picture you never need to look at the same spot twice. In a moving crowd, other characters walk into the area you just checked, and the one you are looking for can slip behind a building and come back out. So the basic rule is: **watch areas, not people.**

| | Still picture | Moving crowd |
|---|---|---|
| The scene | Stays the same | Characters walk around |
| Hidden things | Always hidden | Go behind buildings and walls, then come out |
| Areas you checked | Done for good | May need another look a moment later |
| Advantage | You can take your time | Side views and held items show more clearly while walking |

Think of a crowd search like the classic "find the person in the crowd" books, but in motion. The examples come from [Oddball: Spot the Odd One Out]({{ '/oddball/' | relative_url }}), a game where only one character in a crowd of hundreds is different.

## 5 tips for searching a moving crowd

1. **Watch areas, not people.** While your eyes follow one character, you are not looking anywhere else. Split the screen into two or three areas and quickly scan only the characters in one area at a time.
2. **Sweep in one direction, then come back.** Characters enter and leave while you scan. Sweep from one end to the other, then sweep back the other way on the next pass to close the gaps.
3. **Check behind buildings separately.** A character behind a building or wall cannot be seen from one side. Rotate the map to look from the other side, or wait a moment for characters to walk back out.
4. **Look for what walking reveals.** Walking shows side views and anything held, like a weapon or shield. Scan first for differences you can see from far away, like silhouette and held items, then zoom in later for small ones like the face.
5. **After zooming in, stay put and wait.** When you zoom in close, characters walk off screen easily. Watching one spot for a moment lets you check each character as it passes.

The basics that also work on still pictures, like learning what "normal" looks like first and going from big differences to small ones, are in [7 tips for hidden object and spot the difference games]({{ '/oddball/tips/' | relative_url }}).

## How to use this in Oddball

In Oddball, hundreds of characters fill the town, walk around and hide behind buildings and walls. These features help with a moving crowd:

| Feature | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Zoom** (pinch) | Scan areas from far away; zoom in to check small differences like faces |
| **Rotate the map** (buttons at the bottom left and right) | See characters hiding behind buildings and walls from the other side |
| **Hint: what is different** | Narrow your search to one feature |
| **Hint: roughly where** | Shows a circle around the target. If it walks out of the circle, the game tells you the target moved and shows the circle again |

Hints are unlocked by watching an ad or with gems. The faster you find it, the closer you get to 3 stars.

## FAQ

### Does the one I am looking for move too?

Yes. It walks around with everyone else, which is why the location hint updates when the target moves.

### Can I still find it if it hides behind a building?

Rotate the map to look from the other side, or wait a moment for it to walk back out.

### Is it harder than a still hidden object picture?

It is different more than harder. You may need to look at an area twice, but walking also makes silhouettes and held items easier to see.

### Can I play offline?

Yes. No account is needed, and it works offline.
