# 7 Tips to Get Better at Hidden Object and Spot the Difference Games

<p class="lang-switch">English · <a href="{{ '/oddball/ja/tips/' | relative_url }}" lang="ja">日本語</a> · <a href="{{ '/oddball/es/tips/' | relative_url }}" lang="es">Español</a> · <a href="{{ '/oddball/pt/tips/' | relative_url }}" lang="pt-BR">Português</a> · <a href="{{ '/oddball/ko/tips/' | relative_url }}" lang="ko">한국어</a></p>

In hidden object and spot the difference games, **people with a plan find things faster than people with sharp eyes.** Instead of glancing all over the screen, first learn what everyone has in common, then sweep one area at a time while checking one feature at a time.

The examples come from [Oddball: Spot the Odd One Out]({{ '/oddball/' | relative_url }}), a game where you find the **one character that is different** in a crowd of hundreds. The same tips work for classic spot the difference puzzles and hidden object picture books.

## 7 tips for finding it faster

1. **Learn what "normal" looks like first.** Don't start hunting right away. Spend about five seconds looking at several characters and remember what they all share: head, outfit, color, what they are holding. The odd one only stands out once you have a baseline.
2. **Go from big differences to small ones.** Color → overall silhouette (head shape, whether they carry a weapon or shield) → clothes → face (eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth). Color and silhouette show from far away; faces only show up close.
3. **Check one feature at a time.** Decide "this pass, heads only" and sweep. Comparing several features at once makes your eyes move too fast, and you miss more.
4. **Split the screen and sweep in one direction.** Divide the screen into two or four parts and scan each one left to right, in a zigzag. When your eyes wander freely, you look at the same spots twice and skip others.
5. **Zoom in for small differences.** Eyebrows or a nose are invisible from far away. Zoom in, then move from area to area.
6. **Always check hidden spots.** The odd one often hides behind buildings or walls, at the edge of the screen, or overlapping other characters. In Oddball you can turn the map to see behind buildings.
7. **When stuck, switch features.** After staring at the same feature for a while, your eyes get used to it and slide past the difference. Look away for a moment, or if you were checking heads, check clothes instead.

## The most common differences

In Oddball, the odd one differs in exactly **one** of the following. They are listed from easiest to see from far away.

| What is different | Examples | Visible from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Color | Body, skin, hair, top, arm or leg, hair or horn, decoration color | Far away |
| Silhouette | Body shape, head shape, hair or horns, number of arms, arm or leg shape | A bit closer |
| What they hold | Weapon, shield, hands | A bit closer |
| Clothes | Top, pants, shoes, decoration | Close |
| Face | Number of eyes, eye shape, eyebrows, nose, mouth | Zoomed in |

Faces are the hardest. If you have checked colors and silhouettes from far away and found nothing, zoom in and sweep again looking only at eyes, eyebrows and mouths.

### Swirl stages work differently

In a Swirl Stage everyone is mixed up, so there is no "normal". Instead, look for **the one thing only one of them has, or the one thing only one of them is missing**. For example, everyone has eyebrows except one, or nobody carries a weapon except one. So it is faster to check things that are **either there or not**: eyebrows, nose, weapon, shield, decoration, hair or horns.

## When you are still stuck

Oddball gives you three ways out. Try them in this order.

1. **Zoom**: pinch out to look closer, pinch in to see more. Face differences only show when zoomed in.
2. **Turn the map**: use the buttons at the bottom left and right. Characters walk around and hide behind buildings and walls, so some can't be seen from one direction.
3. **Hints**: two levels.
    - "Tell me what is different": tells you which part differs, such as face, body or weapon. Your search narrows to one row of the table above.
    - "Show roughly where it is": shows a circle the odd one is in. If it walks out of the circle, the hint updates.

Hints are unlocked by watching an ad or spending gems. Stars depend on how fast you find it, so the quicker you are, the closer you get to 3 stars. Harder stages give you more time.

## Practice a little every day

The tips stick after a few rounds of using them on purpose. Short, frequent sessions work better than one long one.

Oddball's **Daily Challenge** gives you a new puzzle every day.

- On the same day, almost everyone gets the same puzzle, so you can compare who finds it faster with friends.
- It has 90 characters, and on Saturdays it is a Swirl Stage.
- You get gems the first time you clear it.

The main game has 6 packs — Heroes, Aliens, Dracula, Faces, Beasts and Knights — with 30 stages each, 180 in total. Each pack has its own characters and map, so moving to a new pack is practice for tip 1: learning "normal" again.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between hidden object and spot the difference?

Hidden object games ask you to find items hidden in a picture. Spot the difference games ask you to find what changed between two pictures. Oddball mixes the two: you find the one character in a crowd that is different from the rest.

### Can there be more than one difference?

No. In each round there is one odd character, and it differs in one way.

### How fast do I need to be for 3 stars?

It depends on the stage. Harder stages give you more time. While you play, the game shows how many stars you will get if you find it now.

### Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. You can play offline, on the subway or on a plane.

### Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play with ads. You can remove ads or buy gems in the app. No account is needed.
