Oddball is a relaxing hidden object game and spot the difference puzzle in one. Drag, pinch and rotate the map, search the crowd, and tap the odd one out. It might have a different head, face, outfit, weapon or color. Look closely!

## How to play

- Search the crowd and find the one character that is different
- Pinch to zoom, and rotate the map to look behind buildings
- Find it fast to earn 3 stars

## Features

- 6 packs to explore: Heroes, Aliens, Dracula, Faces, Beasts and Knights, each with its own map
- 180 stages, 30 in each pack, including tricky Swirl stages
- Characters walk around and hide behind walls and buildings
- Stuck? Use a hint to see what is different, or where to look
- A new Daily Challenge every day
- No account needed, just play
