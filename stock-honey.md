## Average price

Enter the shares you hold (price per share, number) and the shares you will buy. See the new average price, total number of shares and total cost right away.

## Average price list

Enter what you hold and the price you will buy at. The list shows, step by step, how many shares you need and how much it costs to bring your average down (or up) to each price. Change the step size to see finer or wider steps.

## Profit

Enter the price you will sell at and the number of shares to see your rate of return and net profit in advance.

## Good to know

- Works for stocks and cryptocurrencies, including fractional quantities
- Choose how many decimal places to show
- This app is a calculator. It does not give investment advice.
