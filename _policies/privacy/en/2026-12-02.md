HFX Lab ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, as well as international standards such as the GDPR (EU), CCPA (US), and APPI (Japan), where applicable.

This policy applies to all users of our mobile applications distributed globally via the App Store and Google Play.

## 1. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

We process personal information for the following purposes:

- **Service Functionality:** To provide and operate app features.
- **Gift Codes:** In apps with a gift feature (currently Keyring Gacha), to let users send items to each other with a gift code and to make sure each gift is received only once.
- **Personalized Advertising:** To deliver ads tailored to users' interests.
- **Purchase Verification:** To confirm in-app purchases and manage user entitlements.
- **Game Services Integration:** To provide access to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games services (leaderboards, achievements, cloud saves, etc.).
- **Analytics and Service Improvement:** In some of our apps, to understand how features are used, improve the app, and measure advertising performance.

## 2. Types of Data Collected

Depending on the services you use, we may process the following data:

- **Device Information:** Device ID, IP address, OS version, language settings
- **App Usage Data:** Feature usage events (e.g., which features are used and how often), interaction logs, error/diagnostic reports, approximate region (country/city)
- **Purchase Data:** In-app purchase receipts (via RevenueCat)
- **Advertising Identifiers:** For ad targeting and measurement
- **Game Progress & Achievements:** When using Game Center or Google Play Games
- **Gift Feature Data (only when you use the gift code feature):**
  - Anonymous user ID (created by Firebase anonymous sign-in, not linked to your name, email or phone number)
  - Gift records: the gift code, the kind of item and the item, the anonymous IDs of the sender and the receiver, and when the gift was created and received
  - App and device integrity checks (Firebase App Check using Google Play Integrity or Apple App Attest), used only to confirm that requests come from our genuine app. We do not store this information.

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers directly from users.

## 4. Service Providers for the Gift Feature

For the gift code feature, we use the following services to store and process data on our behalf:

**Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase App Check)**

- Purpose: Anonymous sign-in, storing gift records, and confirming that requests come from our genuine app
- Data: Anonymous user ID, gift records, app and device integrity checks
- Location: Gift records are stored in Cloud Firestore in Seoul, South Korea. Sign-in and integrity checks may be processed by Google outside your country.

**Apple (App Attest), Google (Play Integrity)**

- Purpose: Confirming that requests come from our genuine app on your device
- Data: App and device integrity check results

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process personal data under the following lawful bases:

- **Consent:** When you accept data tracking for ads.
- **Performance of a Contract:** To deliver in-app purchases and core features, including gift codes.
- **Legitimate Interests:** To improve our services, prevent fraud, and secure the app.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

## 6. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data.
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure ("Right to be forgotten"):** Ask us to delete your data.
- **Objection / Restriction:** Object to or restrict certain processing.
- **Data Portability:** Receive your data in a transferable format.

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at [hfx.lab@gmail.com](mailto:hfx.lab@gmail.com). Because gift records are anonymous, please include the gift code when you ask about a specific gift so that we can find it.

## 7. Data Retention

Except for the gift feature data below, we do not store personal data directly on our servers.

- **Anonymous user ID:** Automatically deleted after 30 days have passed since it was created.
- **Gift records:** Automatically deleted after 30 days have passed since the gift was created. A gift itself expires 7 days after it is created.

Deletion runs automatically, so it may happen some time after the 30 days have passed.

Third-party services may retain your data according to their policies. Please refer to:

- [Google Data Retention](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention)
- [RevenueCat Privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## 8. International Data Transfers

Our app may be used by users worldwide. As such, data may be processed outside your country, including in countries where data protection laws may differ. We ensure any such transfers are made in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
